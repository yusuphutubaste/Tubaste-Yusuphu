Who I Am

Welcome! I'm Tubaste Yusuphu. I am a creator, speaker, and community advocate driven by a desire to bring clarity, perspective, and encouragement to everyday life.

We live in a world with plenty of noise, but often very little direction. Through my videos and community work, I focus on cutting through distractions and focusing on what genuinely matters: personal responsibility, growth, discipline, and honoring our spiritual and communal roots.

Why I Started Creating Content

Too many people wait for the "ideal moment" to make a change in their lives. They wait for conditions to be perfect, for someone else to step in, or for tomorrow to arrive. But one of my foundational beliefs is simple: "Change starts right now."

Whether addressing practical choices like navigating your college major and career decisions, or reflecting on the difference between what we want versus what we truly need, my goal is to give viewers clear, honest reminders that spark positive action.

Community & Faith at the Center

Individual growth without community is incomplete. Documenting meaningful moments—such as ministry gatherings, baptisms, and fellowship in places like Denver, Colorado—is a core part of what I do.

These celebrations connect us back to faith, culture, and each other. They remind us that no matter where life takes us, we are stronger when we remain united and grounded in our values.

My Core Guiding Philosophy

"Your background doesn't define your ceiling. What matters is the honesty you bring to your daily choices and your willingness to take the next step without waiting for permission."
